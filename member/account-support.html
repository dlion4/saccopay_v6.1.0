Frequently Asked Questions
Navigate to My Loans > Apply New Loan, select your desired product, fill in the amount and purpose, upload required documents, and nominate guarantors if needed. Approval typically takes 1–3 business days. You will receive SMS and email notifications at each stage.
M-Pesa payments may take up to 15 minutes to reflect during peak hours. If it exceeds 30 minutes, check your M-Pesa confirmation message, then raise a ticket under M-Pesa / Payments with the transaction code. Our team will reconcile within 2 hours.
Guarantor changes are only permitted before loan disbursement. Go to your pending loan application, click Manage Guarantors, remove the existing guarantor, and send a new request. The new guarantor must accept within 48 hours.
The minimum monthly savings contribution is KES 1,000. However, to maintain loan eligibility at 3x multiplier, we recommend contributing at least KES 5,000 monthly. You can set up a standing order via M-Pesa PayBill 522522.
Dividends are calculated as a percentage of your share capital at the end of the financial year. The Board declares the rate at the AGM. For FY 2024, the rate is 12.5%. Payments are processed via M-Pesa or bank transfer by March 31.
You may request a withdrawal anytime, but minimum balance rules apply. You must retain at least KES 2,000 as a retention amount. Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours on business days. Large withdrawals above KES 50,000 require SACCO admin approval.
