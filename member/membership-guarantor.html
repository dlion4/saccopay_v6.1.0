How Guarantorship Works
1
Member applies for a loan
The SACCO requires 2 guarantors for loans above KES 100,000 as a safety net.
2
Borrower selects you as guarantor
You receive a notification and have 7 days to accept or decline. Review their profile carefully.
3
You verify with OTP
Your OTP approval is legally binding. A portion of your savings capacity is pledged.
4
If borrower defaults
The SACCO may claim the guaranteed amount from your savings. You can then pursue repayment from the borrower.
5
Upon completion
Once fully repaid, your guarantee is released, capacity restored, and your score improves.
Rongo SACCO Policy

Maximum guarantee: 3.5× your savings. Maximum 5 active guarantorships. Must have own loan in good standing.
