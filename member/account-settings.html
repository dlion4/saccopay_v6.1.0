Deactivate Account

Deactivating your account will temporarily suspend access with the following effects:

  • Prevent all new logins to your account immediately
  • Pause automatic standing orders and repayments
  • Freeze loan applications and guarantor requests
  • Keep your shares, savings, and loan history intact
  • Allow reactivation by visiting a branch or calling support

Delete Account Permanently

This action is irreversible

Deleting your account will permanently close your Sacco membership, forfeit all accumulated shares and savings, write off any guarantor liabilities, and cancel all active loans. You will need to reapply as a new member with fresh KYC if you ever wish to return.
