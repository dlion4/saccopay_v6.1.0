How Dividends Work
1
You Buy Shares
Each share costs KES 100 (admin-set). The more shares you hold, the higher your dividend.
2
SACCO Earns Profits
From loan interest, investments, and service fees throughout the financial year.
3
Board Declares Dividend
At the AGM, the board declares a dividend rate (e.g., 30% of share capital) based on performance.
4
You Receive Payment
Dividend = Your Share Capital × Rate. Paid to your preferred account after withholding tax (5%) deduction.
