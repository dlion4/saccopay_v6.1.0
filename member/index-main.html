Frequently Asked Questions

You can make contributions via M-Pesa paybill, bank transfer, or through the "Contribute" button on your dashboard. The M-Pesa paybill number is 123456 and account number is your member number.

Navigate to the Loans section and click "Apply for Loan". You can borrow up to 3x your total savings. You will need guarantors for loans above KES 50,000.

Dividends are declared at the Annual General Meeting (AGM) held every December and credited to member accounts by January 15 of the following year.
