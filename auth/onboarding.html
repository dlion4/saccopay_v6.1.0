Terms of Service

Last updated: May 2026

1. Platform Usage

SACCOPay serves as an intermediary software provider facilitating digital interactions between SACCOs and their members. We do not hold deposits or disburse loans directly.

2. User Responsibilities

You agree to provide accurate KYC documentation and maintain the security of your account credentials (PIN/Passwords).

3. Liability

SACCOPay is not liable for loan rejections, dividend payout delays, or policy changes enacted by your specific SACCO administration.

Data Privacy Policy

Compliant with the ODPC Kenya Data Protection Act.

1. Data Collection

We collect Personally Identifiable Information (PII) including National ID, KRA PIN, Payslips, and Biometrics strictly for identity verification and loan appraisal.

2. Data Sharing

Your data is shared exclusively with your selected SACCOs, the IPRS for identity checks, and Metropol/TransUnion CRBs for credit scoring.

3. Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of your data or request account deletion (subject to SACCO clearance regulations regarding active loans and guarantees).
