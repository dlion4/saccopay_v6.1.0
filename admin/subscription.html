SACCOPay Service Agreement

1. Services: SACCOPay provides cloud-based SACCO management software as a subscription service. All features described in the selected plan are available during the active contract period.

2. Payment Terms: Monthly billing occurs on the 1st of each month. A 5-day grace period applies before service suspension. PAYG installments are subject to a 1.5% service fee per installment unless auto-debit is enabled.

3. Data Ownership: All member data belongs to your SACCO. SACCOPay does not sell, share, or use your data for any commercial purpose. Full data export is available at any time, free of charge.

4. Uptime SLA: SACCOPay guarantees 99.95% monthly uptime. For each hour below this threshold, a service credit of 1x the hourly rate is applied to your next invoice.

5. Early Termination: Within minimum contract term: 30-day written notice + 10% remaining value fee. After minimum term: 30-day written notice, zero penalty.

6. Governing Law: This agreement is governed by the laws of Kenya. Any disputes shall be resolved through arbitration under the Nairobi Centre for International Arbitration (NCIA) rules.
