Rongo SACCO Complete Guarantorship Mandates
1. Governing Scope & Capacity Thresholds

All valid deposit holdings are dynamically bound to maximum lending indices. Members are permitted to commit inbound coverage exposure up to 300% (thrice) their clear non-withdrawable savings allocations. Exposure limits adjust instantly based on ledger updates processed via SACCOPay gateway networks.

2. Liability Distribution Framework

In the highly atypical scenario of underlying facility default, outstanding principal liabilities are instantly divided proportionately among active verifying guarantors. Attached principal allocations remain locked and completely non-withdrawable until the primary defaulting account is brought back to performing PAR compliance levels.

3. Substitution & Release Timelines

Guarantors retain the absolute right to initiate liability substitution requests via the Member Dashboard at any processing interval. Replacement requires an alternate authorized member accepting equivalent capital risk using secure OTP protocols, or direct allocation bindings directly to non-withdrawable personal savings capital pools. Administrative review requires an operational period of 48 hours.

4. Discretionary Data Visibility Permissions

By validating guaranteed coverages, guarantor personnel retain continuous, persistent access to basic collections performance matrices (PAR status, amortization percentages) linked directly to target underlying loan facilities to ensure complete personal risk tracking compliance.
